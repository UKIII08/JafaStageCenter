One system, three screens

Click once — everyone sees it.

The leader picks a slide in Studio. That same second the congregation sees clean lyrics on the projector, and every musician sees the chords in their own key on their own phone.

Worship leader — Studio

Song library, setlist, section tiles, one-click transpose, blackout and logo. Everything on one screen, designed for Sunday-morning pressure.

Musician — their own phone

Live chords in your notation, capo mode, beginner mode, chord diagrams and private notes. Scan a QR code once — it works every Sunday.

Congregation — projector

Clean lyrics without chords, your church's background and logo, and the CCLI copyright notice added automatically.

Features

Everything your team actually uses.

Keys that fit your vocalists

Every musician saves their preferred key. Building the setlist, you see your vocalist prefers Dm — and transpose in one click.

Song-to-song transitions

A music engine suggests bridge chords from one key to the next — aware of the style and tempo of both songs.

Ambient pads

Atmosphere beds in the key of the current song, with smooth crossfades when the key changes.

Team scheduling

Create a service, the team marks availability, you assign who plays what and attach the setlist. Everyone knows when they play and what to practice.

Conference mode

PDF presentations, Canva embeds, a speaker timer and a presenter view — on the same screens, no cable swapping.

Effortless CCLI reporting

The copyright notice on the projector and an automatic song usage counter. When your reporting period comes, export a CSV — done.

Room for everyone

Make room for musicians who are still learning.

Not everyone on the team is a seasoned player — that's what a team is for. Each person's own key, capo mode, beginner chords and chord diagrams take the pressure off, so someone newer can serve on Sunday instead of dreading it. And the setlist opens every song in the key you'll actually play, with a metronome and private notes for practice during the week.

The tech stays in the background

Rehearsals run long, someone's sick, the setlist changes an hour before. The tools keep working quietly so the team can keep its eyes on what actually matters.

Built inside a worship team

Jonathan was David's friend — and David is the best-known worshiper in history. This is meant to be that kind of friend to you: it was born on a worship team, not in a boardroom.

Your church stays in control

Leader and musician roles, your own logo and background, encrypted connections, and account deletion in one click. Screens connect by QR and are revoked just as easily.
