3. Where your data lives

Our servers are in Warsaw, Poland (OVH) — data stays within the European Economic Area. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as irreversible cryptographic hashes (scrypt), and backups are encrypted (AES-256).

4. Processors

We use OVH (hosting, EU) and — for transactional e-mail — Brevo (EU). We have GDPR-compliant data processing agreements with both.

5. Retention

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, personal data is erased immediately and disappears from backups within 7 days at the latest (backup rotation).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase (the "Delete account" button in settings, or an e-mail to us), restrict processing, and to data portability, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies

We use a single essential session cookie (to keep you signed in). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
